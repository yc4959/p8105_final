## Typical patient profiles

For each condition, we used the most common age group and severity
combination. We used the median length of stay among all eligible stays
for that condition.

| Condition             | Age group   | Severity | Days |
|:----------------------|:------------|:---------|-----:|
| Heart failure         | 70 or Older | Major    |    5 |
| Respiratory infection | 70 or Older | Major    |    5 |
| Stroke                | 70 or Older | Moderate |    4 |
| Sepsis                | 70 or Older | Extreme  |    6 |
| Kidney/UTI            | 70 or Older | Moderate |    4 |
